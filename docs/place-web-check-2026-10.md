# Place catalogue web check, October 2026

On 2026-10-10 all 149 catalogue places were checked against public sources: official sites, UNESCO, the UK and US travel advice pages, travel guides and recent news. This is not a check by locals. Places stay "Not yet checked by a local" until a reviewer confirms them in the app.

## Result

- No place was found permanently closed.
- 11 notes were wrong or out of date and are fixed in this change.
- 1 place was a duplicate and was removed, so the catalogue now has 148 places.
- About 35 "ok" verdicts rest on general knowledge rather than a specific recent page. These are mostly generic entries ("dinner along Bole Road", "local tej bet") and markets.

## Access, as of October 2026

These are in the city safety notes, which the app shows on every trip that includes the city.

- **Amhara** (Lalibela, Gondar, Bahir Dar, Simien): UK and US advise against all travel. Lalibela flights were suspended in September 2026 after fighting near the town.
- **Tigray** (Axum): civilian flights suspended, foreigners barred since May 2025.
- **Afar** (Danakil): foreigners cannot fly into Afar airports; UK advises against travel near the Tigray and Eritrea borders, which covers Dallol.

Sources: [US Embassy security alert, 25 Sep 2026](https://et.usembassy.gov/security-alert-u-s-embassy-addis-ababa-ethiopia-september-25-2026/), [US travel advisory, Apr 2026](https://et.usembassy.gov/travel-advisory-ethiopia-april-2026/), [Australia Smartraveller](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/africa/ethiopia), [Ireland DFA](https://www.ireland.ie/en/dfa/overseas-travel/advice/ethiopia/).
